In part, explicit decisions. In part, inertial drift. But also because tech startups as an industry do not show up in Washington D.C. and in the political system the way big companies do. As long as this imbalance persists, the war on tech startups and the resulting threat to America will continue.\nTherefore the need to politically defend Little Tech.\nOpportunity\nReversing ruinous policies is just one side of the coin.
