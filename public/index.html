How it works

Sample the archive

We look up captures from the Wayback Machine across your date range. This is a sampled history, not every change.

Group similar text

Captures with similar rendered text are grouped together. Use the slider to adjust how closely they must match.

Compare versions

Browse previews, compare text with the previous version, or open a capture in the Wayback Machine.

Know the limits

Archived pages can be incomplete. Dates show when a page was captured, not when a change first went live.
